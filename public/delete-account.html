Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

You can request the deletion of your Anime Store account and all associated personal data at any time. Please follow the steps below.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be removed as described below.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — Delete directly from the app:

  1. Open the Anime Store app on your device
  2. Go to Profile (bottom navigation)
  3. Tap Settings or Edit Profile
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted

Option 2 — Request via email:

  1. Send an email to garidga99@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: "Account Deletion Request – Anime Store"
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. We will process your request within 7 business days

What Data Is Deleted vs. Kept

Data Type Action Retention Period
Name & Email address DELETED Immediately upon deletion
Profile picture DELETED Immediately upon deletion
Delivery address DELETED Immediately upon deletion
Cart & Wishlist data DELETED Immediately upon deletion
Order history KEPT Retained for 90 days for legal & accounting purposes, then deleted
Support chat messages DELETED Immediately upon deletion
