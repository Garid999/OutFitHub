Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Anime Store is built from the ground up for anime fans in Mongolia.

🛍️

Browse by Anime

Filter the catalog by your favorite series — Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, Attack on Titan, and 8 more.

📦

Real-Time Stock

See exactly how many pieces are left in each size before you order. Sold-out sizes are automatically disabled.

❤️

Wishlist

Save items you love and come back to them anytime. Your wishlist syncs across all your devices instantly.

🏷️

Live Discount Events

Time-limited sales and promotions automatically apply to your cart — no coupon codes needed.

🚚

3-Day Delivery

Orders placed in Ulaanbaatar are delivered within 3 calendar days, right to your address.

💬

Direct Support Chat

Have a question? Chat directly with the store admin inside the app and get a fast response.

🌙

Dark & Light Mode

Switch between a sharp dark UI and a clean light theme — your preference is saved automatically.

📋

Order History

Track every order you've placed — items, sizes, prices, and delivery status all in one place.

⭐

Product Ratings

Rate any product with a star rating so the community knows what's worth buying.

About

Built by a fan,
for fans.

Anime Store is a solo project — designed, developed, and shipped by one person.

G

Garid Gantumur

Flutter Developer · Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia

I built Anime Store because I wanted a place where Mongolian anime fans could buy high-quality fashion that actually represents what they love. The entire app — from the real-time Firestore backend to the admin dashboard — was designed and coded from scratch using Flutter and Firebase.
